Sharp Frames Python focus scoring

The experimental target-tiled denoised Laplacian–Tenengrad shadow metric in
src/quality/sharpness.ts is adapted from focus_scoring.py in Sharp Frames
Python: https://github.com/Reflct/sharp-frames-python

The adaptation retains the upstream Gaussian denoising, Laplacian variance,
Tenengrad energy, and equal log-domain weighting. Open Web 3D Capture applies
the metric to its object-centered tile model rather than a whole 512-pixel
image. The experimental score does not currently control frame acceptance.
